Questions, answered.

What exactly is Sworn?

An AI that remembers the goals you tell it about and messages you when you're falling behind, instead of waiting for you to open an app and check.

Where does it message me?

Telegram at launch. It's the fastest way to get this in front of the first users. Other channels are being evaluated based on demand.

How is this different from a reminder app?

A reminder fires on a timer whether or not you needed it. Sworn looks at what you actually did before deciding whether, and how, to reach out.

Can I change the personality later?

Yes, any time, from settings. Nothing about your goals or history resets when you switch.

What happens to my data?

It's used to decide when and how to message you, and nothing else. Full details will ship with the product's privacy policy.
